Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Japan SSW ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (for example, when you create an account, apply for a job, or contact us) and automatically-collected information (such as browser and device data, IP address, and cookies) necessary to provide and improve the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to operate, maintain, and provide the services, to communicate with you about your account and job applications, and to comply with legal obligations. We may also use aggregated, de-identified data for analytics and product improvements.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with third party service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., hosting, analytics, messaging), and with employers when you choose to apply for a job. We will disclose information where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to provide core site functionality and to analyze site usage. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information. However no method of transmission over the internet or storage is 100% secure. Use caution when sharing sensitive personal information.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@japanssw.example (placeholder).
