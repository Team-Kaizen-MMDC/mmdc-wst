Ward Nursing Support

Setagaya Ward (Tokyo) ・ Medical / Nursing ・ Full Time ・ 2025 2025

Conditions

  • ¥184,600–306,100/month
  • Shift system (8 hours actual work, 60-minute break)
    • Day shift: 8:30–17:30
    • Early shift: 7:30–16:30
    • Late shift: 10:00–19:00
  • Days Off:
    • 2–2.5 days off per week
    • Annual holidays: 120 days
    • New Year’s: Dec 29–Jan 3
    • Summer: Aug 13–15
  • Bonus: Twice a year (August, December)
  • Trial Period: 6 months
  • Overtime: Average 10 hours/month
  • Workplace: Setagaya Ward, Tokyo
  • Health Insurance: Paid by company
  • Commuting Expenses: Partially subsidized

Requirements

  • Language Requirements
    Japanese: N3 or above
    English: Not required
  • Visa Type
    Specified Skills Visa
  • Experience
    Experience working in a medical facility preferred

Department Overview

Jhesperus Japan Co., Ltd.

This clinic specializes in orthopedic and rehabilitation treatments. We perform arthroscopic surgery and focus on sports medicine.

This is a workplace where you can put your desire to “help someone” and “contribute to the medical field” into practice. With little overtime and systems for bonuses and salary increases, it’s ideal for those seeking stable, long-term employment.

Why We Hire

We strive daily to ensure that patients who are hospitalized before and after surgery can live their lives with peace of mind. We look forward to receiving applications from people who wish to work alongside us in supporting patient care.

Position Details

You will work as a nurse support staff on the ward, helping maintain a clean, safe, and organized environment.

  • Maintaining the ward environment (bed making, cleaning)
  • Serving and clearing hospital meals
  • Cleaning up, recording, and reporting on daily life and care
  • Cleaning inside the hospital

Mandatory Qualifications

  • Experience working in a medical facility is preferred
