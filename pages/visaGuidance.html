Visa Guidance for Filipinos Applying for SSW 1 & 2

What is SSW?

The Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) visa allows foreign nationals to work in Japan in designated industries. There are two types:

  • SSW 1: For those with skills in specific sectors. Requires passing skills and Japanese language tests.
  • SSW 2: For advanced workers who have experience under SSW 1 and meet additional requirements.

Eligibility for Filipinos

  • Must be a Filipino citizen residing in the Philippines.
  • Pass the required skills and Japanese language exams.
  • Receive a job offer from a Japanese employer.
  • Obtain a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from the Immigration Services Agency of Japan.

Application Steps

  1. Pass the skills and language tests for your chosen industry.
  2. Secure a job offer from a Japanese company.
  3. Your employer applies for a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) in Japan.
  4. Once you receive the COE, prepare the required documents:
  • Visa Application Form (Download PDF)
  • Valid Philippine passport
  • COE (original and copy)
  • Photo (as per embassy requirements)
  • Other documents as required by the Japanese Embassy
  1. Submit your application to the Japanese Embassy or accredited agency in the Philippines.
  2. Wait for processing (usually about one week).
