Department Overview

Located just an 8-minute walk from Sangenjaya Station, we are a locally renowned ramen chain known for our delicious food and friendly service. Our team is made up of individuals from various backgrounds, including many from Vietnam and Nepal.

We value teamwork, inclusivity, and a supportive atmosphere where everyone can grow. Hands-on training is provided to help you succeed, even if you have no prior experience.

Why We Hire

Are you looking for a fulfilling job in a fast-paced and friendly environment? We are hiring dedicated individuals to join our popular ramen shop in Sangenjaya. This is a great opportunity to start a career in the food industry, even if you’re just getting started.

Position Details

  • General hall duties
  • Kitchen cooking and washing support
  • Cashier duties
  • Cheerfully greet customers in Japanese
