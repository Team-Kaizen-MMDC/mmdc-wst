Ward Nursing Support

Tokyo • Nursing Care • Full time • 2025

Conditions

  • ¥184,600 to ¥306,100/month
  • Apply from Anywhere
  • Relocation to Japan (Overseas visa sponsorship is not supported)

Requirements

  • Language Requirements
    Japanese: N3 or above
    English: Not Required
  • Minimum Experience
    Experience working in a medical facility is preferred

Department Overview

Jhesperus Japan Co., Ltd.

This clinic specializes in orthopedic and rehabilitation treatments. We perform arthroscopic surgery, focusing on sports medicine.

This is a workplace where you can put your desire to "help someone" and "contribute to the medical field" into practice.

There is little overtime and a system for bonuses and salary increases, making this the perfect place for those looking for long-term, stable employment!

Why We Hire

We strive every day to ensure that patients who are hospitalized before and after surgery can live their lives with peace of mind.

We look forward to receiving applications from people who would like to work with us.

Position Details

You will work as a nurse support staff on the ward.

  • Maintaining the ward environment (bed making, cleaning)
  • Serving and clearing hospital meals
  • Cleaning up, recording and reporting on daily life and care
  • Cleaning inside the hospital, etc.

Mandatory Qualifications

  • Only people living in Japan can apply
  • Experience working in a medical facility is preferred!
