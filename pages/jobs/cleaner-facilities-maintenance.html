Cleaning Staff

Katsuragawa (Kyoto) ・ Cleaning Services ・ Full time ・ 2025 2025

Conditions

  • ¥9,000/day
  • Work Hours: 08:00–17:00, 3–6 days per week
  • Trial Period: 180 days
  • Transportation: Open to negotiation
  • Other Allowances:
    • Insurance included
    • Commuting by car allowed
    • Commuting by motorbike allowed
    • Work schedule is negotiable
  • Other Benefits:
    • Paid leave
    • Full-time employee recruitment system

Requirements

  • Language Requirements
    Japanese: Conversational / N3
    English: Not required
  • Minimum Experience
    No experience required

Department Overview

Our cleaning department is responsible for maintaining clean and comfortable environments in a variety of settings, including office buildings, stores, and private homes.

We are a close-knit team that values cooperation and clear communication. Team members travel together to 2–3 sites per day, fostering a friendly atmosphere where we share both work and personal stories.

We believe that everyone, regardless of experience, can contribute to our mission of providing exceptional service.

Why We Hire

We are seeking motivated individuals to join our cleaning staff. This is an excellent opportunity for anyone who wants to work in a supportive, flexible environment with a clear path toward stable employment and growth.

Position Details

Perform general cleaning duties at various sites in the Kyoto area.

  • Travel with a team to 2–3 sites per day
  • Clean offices and general housing
  • Communicate with team members and customers
