Construction Assistant

Daimon Sta. (Tokyo) ・ Construction ・ Part-Time ・ 2025 2025

Conditions

  • ¥1,400–2,800/hour
  • Daily Wage: ¥13,000–20,000
  • Work Location: Daimon Sta. (Tokyo)
  • Other Locations: Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama, Chiba
  • Work Hours: 08:00–17:00
  • Time Commitment: 2–3 days/week, less overtime, short-term
  • Perks:
    • Chance to get hired full-time
    • Paid daily
    • Raise and promotion opportunities
    • High earning potential

Requirements

  • Language Requirements
    Japanese: N5
    English: Poor
  • Minimum Experience
    No experience required
    Male or female preferred
    Foreign nationals welcome

Department Overview

We are also involved in various construction businesses that match your skills and goals, so you can find a job that suits you!

Why We Hire

We are hiring Construction Assistants to join our team on demolition sites. This is a great opportunity for anyone who wants to start a career in construction—no prior experience required.

Position Details

You'll primarily be assisting at demolition sites and working alongside specialized staff to keep operations safe and efficient.

  • Assist staff at demolition sites
  • Clear trash and organize materials
  • Water the area to reduce dust
  • Work safely in a supportive, team-based environment
  • Even without physical strength or experience, you can start confidently
  • Occasionally guide people (e.g., to restrooms) in Japanese

Mandatory Qualifications

  • None required
  • Applicants must be motivated and willing to learn
  • Foreign nationals welcome to apply
