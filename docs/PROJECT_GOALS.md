# Project Goals & Target Users

## Executive Summary

The Japan SSW (Specified Skilled Worker) platform is designed to connect international workers with Japanese employers while providing comprehensive visa guidance and support services through Regional Support Organizations (RSOs).

## Target Users

### Primary Users

**1. Job Seekers (SSW Visa Holders/Applicants)**

- International workers seeking employment in Japan under the SSW program
- Current SSW visa holders looking to change employers
- Individuals researching SSW opportunities before applying for visas
- Demographics: Age 18-45, varying Japanese language proficiency (N4-N2), diverse professional backgrounds

**2. Employers Seeking SSW Workers**

- Japanese companies authorized to hire SSW workers
- HR departments and recruitment managers
- Small to large enterprises across 14 specified skill sectors
- Need: Streamlined access to qualified international talent

**3. Immigration Agencies/Consultants**

- Licensed immigration consultants and legal advisors
- Educational institutions with SSW programs
- Regional Support Organizations (RSOs)
- Need: Platform to connect services with target users

### Secondary Users

**4. Government Officials & Regulators**

- Immigration services monitoring compliance
- Labor standards officials
- Regional government representatives

**5. Support Service Providers**

- Language training institutions
- Housing and relocation services
- Cultural orientation providers

## Success Metrics

### Primary KPIs

**User Engagement**

- Monthly active users (target: 10,000+ within 6 months)
- Average session duration (target: 5+ minutes)
- Pages per session (target: 4+ pages)
- Return visitor rate (target: 40%+)

**Job Application Metrics**

- Search-to-application conversion rate (target: 15%+)
- Job application completion rate (target: 80%+)
- Time from search to application (target: <10 minutes)
- Application-to-interview conversion (target: 25%+)

**Platform Utilization**

- Job postings published monthly (target: 500+)
- Company profile completeness (target: 90%+)
- RSO directory usage (target: 1,000+ monthly views)
- Visa guidance page engagement (target: 70% completion rate)

### Secondary KPIs

**User Satisfaction**

- User feedback scores (target: 4.2+ out of 5)
- Support ticket resolution time (target: <24 hours)
- Feature adoption rates for new releases

**Business Impact**

- Successful job placements facilitated
- Employer satisfaction with candidate quality
- Platform revenue growth (if applicable)

## Feature Scope

### Core Platform Features

**1. Job Search and Filtering**

- Advanced search by industry, location, salary, visa status
- Filter by Japanese language requirements (N5-N1)
- Sort by relevance, date posted, salary, location proximity
- Save searches and set up job alerts
- Multi-language support (English, Japanese, others as needed)

**2. Company Profiles and Discovery**

- Comprehensive company information and culture insights
- Open positions listing with direct application links
- Employee testimonials and workplace photos
- Company size, benefits, visa sponsorship details
- Location maps and transportation access information

**3. RSO (Regional Support Organizations) Directory**

- Searchable database of verified RSOs by region
- Service offerings and specializations
- Contact information and consultation booking
- User reviews and ratings
- Integration with job postings showing relevant RSO support

**4. Visa Guidance Resources**

- Step-by-step SSW visa application process
- Document requirements and templates
- Timeline expectations and processing updates
- FAQ section addressing common concerns
- Legal compliance and regulation updates

**5. User Profile Management**

- Personal information and visa status tracking
- Resume/CV builder with Japanese formatting
- Application history and status tracking
- Skill assessments and certifications upload
- Language proficiency verification

### Enhanced Features (Future Phases)

**6. Communication Platform**

- In-platform messaging between users and employers
- Interview scheduling and calendar integration
- Notification system for application updates

**7. Analytics and Reporting**

- User dashboard with application analytics
- Employer dashboard with candidate pipeline metrics
- Platform usage analytics for administrators

**8. Mobile Application**

- iOS and Android native apps
- Push notifications for job matches
- Offline access to saved content

## Project Scope Boundaries

### In Scope

- Job search and application functionality
- Company and RSO directory services
- Visa guidance and information resources
- User account management and profiles
- Multi-language content support
- Basic communication features

### Out of Scope (Initial Release)

- Payment processing for premium services
- Real-time chat or video calling
- Background check or verification services
- Immigration document preparation tools
- Legal advisory services beyond information

### Future Considerations

- Integration with government systems
- Advanced AI-powered job matching
- Comprehensive employer verification system
- Extended multi-language support (10+ languages)

## Success Definition

**Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Success:**

- 1,000+ registered users within 3 months
- 100+ active job listings across key industries
- 50+ verified company profiles
- Complete RSO directory with contact information
- Functional job search and application process

**Growth Phase Success (6-12 months):**

- 10,000+ monthly active users
- 500+ monthly job applications submitted
- 90%+ user satisfaction score
- Expansion to additional SSW industries
- Partnership agreements with major RSOs

**Long-term Success (1-2 years):**

- Market leadership in SSW job placement platform
- Sustainable revenue model established
- Government recognition and partnership
- International expansion to other work visa programs
- Platform ecosystem with integrated service providers

## Risk Mitigation

**User Adoption Risks:**

- Comprehensive marketing and outreach strategy
- Partnership with existing immigration services
- Multi-language support from launch

**Content Quality Risks:**

- Verification process for job postings and company information
- Regular content audits and updates
- User feedback and reporting mechanisms

**Regulatory Compliance Risks:**

- Legal review of all platform content
- Regular updates to reflect policy changes
- Consultation with immigration law experts
