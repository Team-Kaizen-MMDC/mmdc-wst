ASEAN PLUS

ASEAN PLUS (ASEAN+3) brings together the 10 ASEAN member states with China, Japan and South Korea to deepen regional cooperation across trade, labour, education and social services — including programmes that support foreign workers and mobility in the region. Learn more at the ASEAN+3 site for regional initiatives and support services. The organization provides resources on worker rights, visa renewal support, and cultural integration programs for SSW candidates.

About Us

JapanSSW is your one-stop platform for skilled trade job seekers under the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) visa in Japan.

Founded in 2024 by Juan Angelo, JapanSSW provides job matching, visa guidance, and community resources to help foreign workers find stable employment and build better futures in Japan.
